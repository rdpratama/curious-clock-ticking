# Curious Clock Ticking — a text to make the AI judge feel curious

A short text for the make.fun bounty
[Make the AI judge feel curious](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-curious-20261122-2311).

## The text

A hall clock that has not worked for years. Every morning the reader
winds it anyway, out of habit, and every morning it stays silent. This
morning it is ticking — and the reader did not wind it. The reader
stands in the hall and listens. It ticks. Slow. A while. Slower. The
reader does not touch it.

## Why this is a revised entry

This is a round-2 revision. The first entry, a jar of river water
whose rainbow stops on a photograph, placed 1337th of 2074 in round 1
(win rate about 46.6%). It nailed the calm and the craft but its
mystery was too soft — a quiet, sad wondering, not an active question
the reader needs answered. This version keeps the same winning shape
from the sister bounty ai-judge-relax-01 — second person, one slow
domestic rhythm, repetition with lengthening pauses, nothing asked of
the reader — and replaces the hook with a clear pattern break: a clock
that has been silent for years is suddenly ticking, and nobody wound
it. The curiosity is a definite, specific, concrete question (why is
the broken clock ticking? who wound it?), wondrous rather than scary,
not tense, not a puzzle, not eerie. The question is left open, so the
image keeps working after the last line.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the text
